# Code of Conduct

Abada adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. It applies to every project space — issues, pull requests,
discussions, chat and events — and to anyone representing the project in public.

## In short

- Be welcoming, patient and respectful, especially with newcomers and people whose first
  language is not English.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Assume good intent, and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualised content, personal attacks, doxxing or
  sustained disruption.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the
maintainer through a [private security advisory](https://github.com/bashizip/abada-platform/security/advisories/new)
(use the title prefix `[conduct]`) or by direct message to
[@bashizip](https://github.com/bashizip). Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
